## What this app does

All Video Downloader downloads a video file from a web link that you paste into the app, saves it
to your device, and lists your saved files so you can open them in a video player of your choice.

## Information we collect

**None.** The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting
service. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

Specifically, the app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts,
location, device identifiers, advertising ID, or browsing history.

## Information that stays on your device

The following data is created on your device and never leaves it:

- **The link you paste.** Used only to fetch the file you asked for. It is not stored after the
  download finishes and it is not sent anywhere except to the server hosting that file.
- **Downloaded video files.** Saved to the public `Movies/AllVideoDownloader` folder on your
  device so that your gallery and other player apps can see them. You can delete them from inside
  the app or with any file manager.
- **A one-time setting** recording that you acknowledged the app's usage notice, and the
  identifiers of downloads currently in progress. Both are stored in the app's private
  preferences.

## Network activity

The app makes network requests to exactly one kind of destination: the web address you paste. It
first sends a lightweight request to read the file's name, size, and type, then downloads the file
using the Android system download service. The app contacts no servers operated by us, because we
operate none.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- **Internet / network state** — to download the file from the link you provide.
- **Read video files on your device** (`READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, or `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on
  Android 12 and older) — so the Library screen can list videos you have downloaded, including
  files saved by an earlier installation of the app. The app reads only video files, never photos,
  documents, or other data. You can decline this permission and the rest of the app keeps working.

The app does **not** request all-files access, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or the
ability to install other apps.

## Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

## Content and copyright

The app downloads only from direct links that you supply, and it refuses links from services whose
terms of service prohibit third-party downloading. You are responsible for having the right to
download and store the content you request. Do not use this app to copy material you do not own or
do not have permission to save.

If you believe content is being distributed in a way that infringes your rights, please note that
we host no content whatsoever — contact the operator of the website hosting the file.

## Data deletion

Because no data is collected, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the app
created on your device, delete your videos from the Library screen and uninstall the app.
